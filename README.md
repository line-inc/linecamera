# 📷 Line Camera - Professional Camera App for Android

**Line Camera** is a modern, high-performance Android camera application engineered with Jetpack Compose, Kotlin, and the native Android Camera2 API. Designed for photo enthusiasts and cinematographers alike, Line Camera delivers professional manual controls, high-bitrate video recording, RAW capture, and intuitive hardware controls.

---

## 🚀 Unique Features Other Camera Apps Don't Have

- 🎞️ **OpenGate 4:3 Video Mode**: Record full-sensor 4:3 video (`1440x1080`), giving you complete flexibility for anamorphic lens squeezing and vertical/horizontal post-production cropping.
- 🎙️ **Real-Time Stereo L/R Peak Meters**: View active 48kHz dual-channel stereo microphone levels and adjust audio gain directly on the main camera interface.
- ⚡ **Ultra-High 160 Mbps Bitrate Engine**: Record cinema-quality HEVC and H.264 video with maximum bitrate options up to **160 Mbps** (far exceeding standard camera apps).
- 🎛️ **Xiaomi-Style Animated Zoom Ring**: Intuitive pop-in curved arc zoom dial with butter-smooth 250ms interpolated transitions between **0.6x**, **1.0x**, **2x**, **3x**, **5x**, **10x**, **30x**, and **100x** lens presets.
- 📐 **Dual-Axis Gyro Spirit Level**: Real-time dual-axis (pitch & roll) angle indicators overlaid on the viewfinder to maintain a perfectly level horizon.
- 🔍 **Flagship Autofocus Tuning**: Specialized single-shot and continuous autofocus engine tuned specifically for multi-camera flagships like the Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra and POCO devices without constant lens hunting.
- 🎚️ **Manual Focus Diopter Control**: Fine-tune focus distance using an exact diopter slider for macro and manual focus lock.

## ✨ Full Feature Overview

### 🎥 Cinema & Video Recording
- **Ultra-High Bitrate Recording**: Customize bitrate from **33Mbps** up to **160Mbps** for uncompressed, pristine video capture.
- **Advanced Codec Support**: Choose between **HEVC (H.265)** for maximum compression efficiency and **H.264** for broad compatibility.
- **Multiple Resolutions & Aspect Ratios**: Record in **4K UHD**, **1080p FHD**, **720p HD**, and **OpenGate 4:3 (1440x1080)**.
- **Frame Rate Control**: Switch seamlessly between **30 FPS** and **60 FPS** high-frame-rate recording.
- **Stereo Microphone Level Meter**: Real-time stereo L/R audio gain visualizer with customizable audio gain control.

### 📸 Professional Still Photography
- **Photo & RAW Sensor Capture**: Shoot standard JPEG or uncompressed **DNG RAW** images for maximum post-processing dynamic range.
- **Touch-to-Focus & Exposure Metering**: Accurate single-tap spot metering and autofocus target overlay.
- **Smooth Lens Zoom Ring**: Xiaomi-style curved arc dial with quick lens presets (**0.6x**, **1.0x**, **2x**, **3x**, **5x**, **10x**, **30x**, **100x**) and butter-smooth 250ms animated zoom transitions.

### 🎛️ Pro Manual Controls Panel
- **Manual Focus Diopter Slider**: Dedicated **Default AF**, **Continuous AF**, and **Manual Focus** with precise diopter distance adjustment (`0.00` diopters to macro).
- **ISO & Shutter Speed**: Manual ISO adjustment (up to ISO 19,500+) and Shutter Speed control (`1/1000s` to extended exposure).
- **White Balance Presets**: Daylight, Cloudy, Incandescent, Fluorescent, Twilight, and Auto AWB.
- **Exposure Compensation (EV)**: On-the-fly EV metering compensation slider.
- **Stabilization & Processing Tools**: Optical Image Stabilization (**OIS**), Electronic Image Stabilization (**EIS**), Lens Distortion Correction, High-Quality Noise Reduction, and Sharpening toggles.

### 📐 Overlays & Interface Design
- **Spirit Level & Pitch/Roll Overlay**: Real-time dual-axis gyro level sensor keeping your horizons perfectly straight.
- **Framing Grids**: 3x3 rule of thirds and Golden Ratio compositional grids.
- **Responsive Landscape & Portrait Layouts**: Control UI elements dynamically adapt to rotation with controls anchored comfortably on the right rail in landscape mode.
- **Gallery Shortcut**: Quick thumbnail preview linking directly to the latest captured media.
